B-166 Home, 2026-10-30, five drafts. v1 (a setup guide card with a vertical step list and the open step's drop zone, side cards for the company and people) got "not terrible, could be a lot nicer". I then redrew from scratch each time: v2 (the person page's band on Home with a horizontal stepper as its tabs, chart preview in a drop zone) "doesn't work for the type of surface"; v3 (tinted lead card, ghost mini chart, numbered connected stepper, big section titles) "waaaay too vibe coded"; v4 (a calm Notion/Linear column of small labelled lists) "still sucks". Then: "the nicest one was the first one, we went downhill from there; I liked some of the visual elements of v2, not the stepper, that looked so vibecoded". v5 went back to v1's layout with v2's chart preview (org chart cards with faces and counts, playable import), the logo shown large and label-over-value facts.

**Why:** "could be nicer" was a polish note, not a rejection; each from-scratch redraw lost what worked. Horizontal steppers, tinted feature cards with coloured borders, decorative ghost illustrations and amber on many elements read as generated; so does a bare, low-content column.

**How to apply:** when the user says a version is "not bad but could be nicer", keep its structure and refine; after a rejection, ask or infer which elements they liked and carry them, rather than starting over. Never use steppers or tinted hero cards in Fusion mocks. Real product references help with polish, not as a replacement for a structure the user already liked ([[person-pages-lively-not-document]], [[design-governance-antivibecode]], [[canvas-first-for-taste-rework]]).
