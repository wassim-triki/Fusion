How others shape it

BambooHR

"Add employee" opens one long page in the shape of the profile: personal, job, compensation, with a switch for self-service access. Before the start date, a new hire packet tells the person when to arrive, where to go and who to meet, and "Get to know you" introduces them to the team.

We take: the first day as a moment the admin can see before saving.

Personio

A short creation form whose fields are the company's own attributes; mandatory ones are asked up front. Onboarding steps and the invitation hang off the start date and run by themselves.

We take: the company's required fields join the form without a separate screen.

HiBob

"New hire" is a guided wizard (basic info, work, payroll and more) ending in a summary. You choose when the person is invited (now or on the start date) and onboarding task lists start from the hire.

We take: invite timing in the flow, with the tasks shown as they will happen.

ParityNames, contact, number, job, unit, place, manager, employment type, first day, the company's required fields, invite now or on the first day.
