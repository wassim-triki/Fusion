## Demo presets (B-095)

Five presets, each its own Carthage tenant at one point of the performance year, built through the product API at the gateway (`:5000`; the shell's `/api` forwards there unchanged, but its dev server is ~20× slower and drops connections under a build's load) with the Development-only demo clock (`X-Demo-Clock`, Performance refuses it outside Development). Every date counts from the day the build runs, so a preset shows the same situation whatever day it is built; rebuild it before a demo so its "today" is the demo day. Today's Carthage Digital Group tenant above is unchanged and keeps its own scripts.

| Preset | Tenant | What it shows on the build day |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `nextcycle` | Carthage · Next cycle | This year ended 2 days ago: calibrated, released, mostly acknowledged and closed; next year's cycle in Draft from tomorrow, ready to activate. |

Every preset but `fresh` also holds last year's cycle, closed with released results (Aicha rated Strong), and, across both years, 1:1s (Mariam ↔ Aicha every two weeks with one due today, Mariam ↔ Amira weekly, Amira ↔ Omar monthly), feedback, praise and development goals. Notifications older than a week are read. No AI output is seeded. The story isn't uniform on purpose: about one person in eleven (never a persona) skips a check-in round, two in five align a second objective, a few manager reviews land a day late in released rounds, and ratings spread about 10/30/45/13/2% from 5 to 1 (held at 3 with an off-track objective; Omar's team +1).

Commands (stack up, Performance in Development):

- `node .local-docs/agents/data/demo-preset.mjs list`: what is built, when, at which domain.
- `node .local-docs/agents/data/demo-preset.mjs build <preset>`: builds it; does nothing if it is already built.
- `node .local-docs/agents/data/demo-preset.mjs tidy`: archives every retired or failed preset tenant still in the platform list.
- `node .local-docs/agents/data/demo-preset.mjs reset <preset>`: builds a new one, then retires the previous one. If the new build fails, the previous one stays as it was.
- `node .local-docs/agents/data/journey-demo-preset.cjs <preset>`: read-only checks that the preset reached its state, plus the demo clock's refusals.
- `node .local-docs/agents/data/screens-demo-presets.cjs [preset ...]`: the slice 4 and 6 boards only presets reach, dark 1440×900 through the shell (changes no data).

Timing through the gateway: `fresh` under a minute, `planning` about 3 minutes, the others 4–6. Builds run detached-safe: one dropped call or gateway blip is retried; a build that dies leaves a "· build <n>" tenant that the next build of that preset retires; never run two builds of the same preset at once.

Built 2026-10-22 (all five journeys pass, 82 checks): `fresh-5`, `planning-8`, `midyear-5`, `yearend-2`, `nextcycle-2`.

Accounts: all 100 people of each preset are activated through the invite flow with the local password, at `<first>.<last>@<preset>-<n>.example.test`, where `n` counts that preset's builds (accounts are global, so every build needs its own domain). `list` and the build output print the current domain; personas are Amira Khelifi (tenant administrator, HR, manager), Mariam Gharbi (manager, reviews Aicha), Omar Mansour (manager without `core.team.view`, rates generously), Aicha Bouazizi, Leila Baccouche, Khaled Sassi and Rim Mahjoub (employees).

Retired tenants: Fusion has no tenant deletion, so a reset renames the previous tenant "Carthage · <Preset> · retired <date> #<n>" and archives it; a failed build is renamed "· failed …" and archived. Archived tenants leave the platform tenant list but stay in the local databases (their record opens by URL). Tenants retired before archival existed (renamed and deactivated) are archived by the next build of their preset. `demo-presets.local.json` records the presets, their tenants, cycles, employees and the retired ones; `demo-presets/.work/` holds the generated import workbooks. Both are local only.
