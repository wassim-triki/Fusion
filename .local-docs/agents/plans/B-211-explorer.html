What the research says

      
    
        
  • BambooHR keeps a quick search in its top bar that finds a person by name and opens their profile; browsing people is the Directory's job (Quick Search Box, new interface). The search is a fast way to a person, not a report builder.
    • 
        
  • Superhuman, Linear, Slack: one shortcut everywhere (Ctrl/⌘ K); results grouped by kind; things findable by the words people use, not the menu's names (Superhuman's guide).
    • 
        
  • Palette patterns: show recent and nearby things before anything is typed, a short list, not everything; say the scope when it narrows; rank names before descriptions (SaaSFrame patterns, GitLab's palette work).
    • 
        
  • HiBob and Workday mix people, tasks and pages in one box; reviewers praise finding colleagues fast and complain when results bury people under records.
    • 
      

      
What Fusion adds

      
    
        
  • People first, with faces and what they are to you: your manager, on your team, reports to you.
    • 
        
  • Each person finds only what they may open: Aicha Bouazizi, an employee, never sees Jobs, Reports or company settings; nothing is shown that would be refused.
    • 
        
  • A preview to act from: praise, a 1:1 or a change for that person without leaving the page you're on.
    • 
        
  • Forgiving matching: name, job, team, place, employee number or email; accents and case don't matter (Aïcha finds Aicha).
    • 
      

      
How it would work

      
    
        
  • Core HR answers people, teams, places and jobs from one new read, scoped the way the org chart's search already is; Performance answers objectives, 1:1s and reviews the viewer can open. The shell asks both at once as you type and shows Core HR's results first; if one is down, its group is just missing.
    • 
        
  • Pages and actions come from the rights the shell already knows. Recent items stay on the device, per person and company.
    • 
        
  • Tests: each read's scope per role, and another company's people never found.
