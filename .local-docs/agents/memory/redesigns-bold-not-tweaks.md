User, 2026-11-09, on the person page shown in the B-397 colour study: "I don't understand why you keep separating the right column cards from the rest of the page … you're taking the already established thing and just tweaking it, there's no bold design decision whatsoever."

**Why:** the user wants the redesigns (Performance, B-390, and Core HR surfaces under B-396) to look newly designed, not recoloured versions of what exists; separate cards stacked in a right column is the pattern they now read as tweaking.

**How to apply:** for a surface marked rethink or redesign, start from the surface's job and propose layouts that differ in structure (how content flows, what leads, what merges), not card arrangements of today's page. This differs from [[vibecode-tells-tinted-hero-cards]] ("refine the liked version, don't redraw"): that applies to a version the user just accepted in a review round; this applies when the user has called for a redesign.
