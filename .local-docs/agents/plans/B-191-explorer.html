Sign-in

Step 1 asks for the email. Step 2 shows only the ways in for that address. Fusion decides them by the address's domain, never by whether an account exists.

    Invitation

    The invitation link is the proof that someone was invited. The provider must sign them in with the invited address; then the account is made and they're in, with no password to choose.

      Settings › Sign-in

      A "Single sign-on" block above the invitation settings. Anyone who can see Sign-in settings sees it; only administrators change it.
