# Reports engine (B-189 / B-190)

`CoreHR/Features/Reports/ReportEngine` serves standard reports, the builder, both exports and B-190’s front page. `ReportViewer.From` takes authority only from claims. The existing `core.overview.view` read grant now permits Tenant or DirectReports (keeps existing roles working); `core.reports.build` controls saved-definition writes. Reports area's See/Build × team/everyone maps to those grants.

Current manager branch is resolved by `ChangeSupport.BranchAsync` before loading report people or aggregating. That same allowed branch applies to past dates. Dated employment, assignments and unit states supply historical snapshots; custom Fields values are current, since the Fields feature stores current values. Open roles follow Organization's current-plan/from-today rule.

Never bypass `FieldsAsync`'s whitelist, including for grouping and filters. A saved definition may itself hold sensitive filter literals: lists return summaries; opening a definition checks visibility before returning it. Saved reports carry owner and role shares, never authority or cached results. Role audience is read live over the signed Identity channel; sharing does not grant Reports access.

`data/journey-b189-reports.cjs` sets Managers to See their team and keeps the shared Engineering by place demo in Carthage.

Reports review (B-251): configuration stays separate from operations; Fields is beside grouping/dates, Save is primary in the tenant theme, lighter toolbar controls per the user. Reuse FilterFieldChip (Organization/People pattern) and the shared DateRangePicker, not text filters or nested single-date pickers. Standard Share saves and shares in one flow; partial-success retry retains the saved report.

B-190 adds `GET /corehr/reports/front-page` through `ReportEngine.FrontPageAsync` / private `RunCoreAsync`: the same scoped row evaluation, with monthly points plus actual employment, assignment, manager and unit boundaries. The internal front-page projection is not a builder/export and is not capped at 120 periods. `FrontPageProjection` stays private; callers cannot choose a scope or bypass field policy. The projection captures a snapshot at every boundary but sends each person once with dated stretches (`spans`, `to` exclusive; B-284: 5.5 MB → 120 KB for Carthage); frontend scrubbing, events and Replay rebuild from them locally; the lower next-30-days, required details and open roles stay anchored to today.

D-30’s company departments use the nearest dated Division ancestor where that level exists (Carthage’s six groups, above Department A/B). Companies without Divisions use Department; without either level they fall back to Reports’ grouping. People above that level (a division's own team, the company root) form one "Not in a department" group, last and neutral; their `parent` carries the unit they sit in (B-190 review). Reports’ existing `division` field still means the top ancestor under the organization root; the front page intentionally uses D-30’s middle tier for its company departments. Card leads use dated in-scope manager links (team-only views label them as in scope). Optional job/place/start dates are omitted by the engine whitelist; the People grouping controls follow those fields. `EmployeeFieldService.VisibleRecordsAsync` accepts only the engine’s active allowed ids and visible definitions, with separate Personal/Job audiences, and shares the required-field checker with Settings. Required source Personal details now count there too.

`data/journey-b190-front-page.cjs` is read-only (HR, current manager branch across all dates, no access, other tenant, standard-report count parity). `data/screens-b190-front-page.cjs` captures Growth, People and the supporting sections and exercises grouping, the slider, Today and Replay, dark 1440×900 via the shell.
