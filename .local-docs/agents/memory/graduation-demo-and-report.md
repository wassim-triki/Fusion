The final demo is in front of a graduation jury, technical and not. That doesn't make the demo story the driver: the product is a real HRIS (or modules of one), not dumbed down, and its design should explain itself; the product tour (B-090) comes after.

Time is not the constraint: code is generated fast, so scope is set by what's right, never by the demo date. Don't ask for dates or trim depth to fit a timeline.

The user's graduation report will include a study of existing solutions (not drafted as of 2026-10-30; likely the well-known HRIS products) and how Fusion builds on, changes or improves them; the AI-assisted org import is the precedent they cite.

**Why:** said 2026-10-30 while shaping B-164 (tenant experience overhaul): "let's not dumb it down, but actually let the design be self-explanatory", "stop caring about the demo timeline".

**How to apply:** design for a real HRIS that reads on its own; in product research cover the canonical products and record for each feature whether Fusion is at parity, improves on it (and why) or does something new; improvements are the wow candidates, not copies.
