How others do it

BambooHR

Time off is its own module with balances and requests; the person page shows "Out" with the return date, and Who's Out lists the week. Access levels are per employee group, scoped to "employees who report to them" or to everyone.

We take: "Out until" with the return day wherever the person shows, without building balances.

HiBob

A lifecycle status "On leave" with effective dates sits beside Employed and Terminated, and permission groups can reach "people in my department and below". The employee's activity log keeps account events.

We take: leave as a dated state, and reach by department subtree.

Personio

Absences carry a type; who sees the type is a permission (colleagues see only "absent"). Notification emails carry the company's logo and colour.

We take: the type stays with HR and the manager; emails in the company's brand.
