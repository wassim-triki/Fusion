Don't hand the user everything in the conversation. Store what's worth keeping (board, `data/README.md`, memory) and reply with what happened in a line or two and one concrete next thing.

**Why:** user, 2026-11-06: "You don't need to give me everything in this conversation. I won't remember it anyway... Just give me a concrete thing we can move on to." Also "good enough is good enough": no perfecting outside the main demo journey.

**How to apply:** end with one recommended next step, not a menu. Keep only what they must decide or would be surprised by. Related: [[minimize-eta-no-fluff]], [[name-items-not-just-ids]].
